Monetary Policy Toolkit

Seven-day reverse-repurchase operations

Key short-term policy and liquidity signal in the current framework.

Reserve requirement ratio

Changes the reserve constraint and liquidity position of banks.

Medium-term lending and other liquidity facilities

Provide term funding and influence bank funding conditions.

Open-market operations

Manage system liquidity and guide money-market rates.

Macroprudential and structural tools

Target credit allocation and financial-stability objectives alongside aggregate monetary conditions.

How Monetary Policy Affects the Economy

Policy rates + liquidity + reserve tools→Money-market & bank funding costs→Loan pricing & credit supply→Investment / property / consumption→Activity & employment→Prices

Transmission also operates through expectations, asset prices, credit conditions and—where especially important—the exchange rate.

What to monitor

Why the Balance Sheet Matters

What Could Change Policy Next?

Inflation / tighter-policy pressure

Persistent inflation, stronger wage or demand pressure, adverse energy/import-price shocks, or rising inflation expectations.

Growth / easier-policy pressure

Materially weaker activity, labour-market deterioration, tighter credit conditions or a faster and sustained decline in underlying inflation.

These are decision-relevant conditions, not a prediction of the next policy action.
