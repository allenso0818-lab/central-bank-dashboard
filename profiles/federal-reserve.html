Analysis & Policy Outlook

Inflation risk

Inflation remains above the 2% longer-run goal. The September SEP median put 2026 PCE inflation at 3.7%, with participants overwhelmingly assessing inflation risks as tilted to the upside.

Labour & growth balance

The September SEP median projected 2026 real GDP growth of 2.3% and unemployment of 4.1%. A resilient labour market reduces pressure for immediate easing, while deterioration in payrolls, claims or participation would shift the balance.

Policy trajectory

The September SEP median federal funds rate was 4.1% for end-2026. Current futures pricing also points to meaningful odds of further tightening. These are separate signals: the dot plot is participant projections; FedWatch is market pricing.

Scenario analysis, not a recommendation or certainty. Key swing factors: PCE/core PCE, payrolls, unemployment, wages, claims, GDP/demand, oil and inflation expectations.

Data Sources & Methodology

Labour-market and GDP charts load public FRED CSV series sourced primarily from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and Bureau of Economic Analysis. The countdown uses the Federal Reserve's published FOMC calendar and standard 2:00 p.m. ET statement release time. Dot-plot data come from the Federal Reserve September 2026 Summary of Economic Projections. FedWatch probabilities are attributed to CME FedWatch; displayed percentages are dated public snapshots, with a direct link to CME for live pricing.
