Decision rules, evidence timing and limitations
  • The latest statement has first priority. A meaningfully hawkish or dovish change moves 5 percentage points between Increase and Hold; a strongly aligned statement and post-meeting Chair/Governor communication can move at most 10. This is a disclosed sensitivity convention, not a statistically calibrated likelihood.
  • Minutes and speeches only move the next-meeting estimate when released after the latest decision and relevant to monetary policy. Older minutes remain context; unrelated speeches receive zero adjustment.
  • Inflation and labour each require at least two fresh, consistent observations after the latest meeting before a 5-point change is considered. Missing core PCE, CPI, or current payroll series cannot be inferred. Growth confirms the signal; SEP validates the medium-term path and has zero mechanical next-meeting weight.
  • Every adjustment transfers probability between outcomes; totals stay 100%. The displayed whole percentages are rounded from the verified market anchor and the explicit adjustments. No numeric outlook is shown if the FedWatch snapshot is missing or older than seven days.
Outlook methodologyDashboard probability analysis uses the verified FedWatch snapshot plus the SEP, inflation, labour-market and growth information shown above. Increase / Remain the same / Decrease probabilities sum to 100%. They are scenario estimates, not official FOMC probabilities.
