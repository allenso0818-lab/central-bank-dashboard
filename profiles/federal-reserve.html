How Monetary Policy Affects the Economy

Federal funds rate→Money-market rates→Bank and market borrowing costs→Consumption & investment→Employment & activity→Inflation

Transmission also operates through expectations, asset prices, credit conditions and—where especially important—the exchange rate.

What Could Change Policy Next?

Inflation / tighter-policy pressure

Persistent inflation, stronger wage or demand pressure, adverse energy/import-price shocks, or rising inflation expectations.

Growth / easier-policy pressure

Materially weaker activity, labour-market deterioration, tighter credit conditions or a faster and sustained decline in underlying inflation.

These are decision-relevant conditions, not a prediction of the next policy action.
